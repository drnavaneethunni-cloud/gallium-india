Which performance characteristics are assessed?
  • Filtration efficiency and dirt-holding capacity
  • Burst and collapse pressure
  • Seal and leak integrity
  • Flow rate and pressure drop
  • Vibration and thermal endurance

The applicable tests and performance parameters vary by filter category, part number and operating conditions.
